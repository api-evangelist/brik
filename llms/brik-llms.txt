# Brik

> Brik ("[Re]Constructing Construction") is an Indonesian construction-materials
> company that operates concrete batching plants and supplies ready-mix concrete
> for building projects across the Greater Jakarta region. Backed by Accel. Brik
> does not currently publish a public developer API, OpenAPI specification, SDKs,
> or webhook surface; this profile is maintained in the API Evangelist network as
> a company record.

## Company
- [Brik website](https://brik.id/): Ready-mix concrete supply, batching plants, and project delivery.
- [Brik articles](https://brik.id/articles/): Company articles and project updates.

## Coverage
- Service area: Greater Jakarta — Bekasi, Bogor, Karawang, and Bandung.
- Products: standard ready-mix concrete and "Super Concrete" variants; mass-concrete pours.

## APIs
- None published. No public REST/GraphQL API, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI specification, or SDKs were found at the time of enrichment.
